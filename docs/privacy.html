Privacy

This page explains what Daily Cash stores and where. Last updated 8 November 2026.

What the app stores

  • Your name, your email address and your password. The password is handled by the login service and is not readable by the app owner.
  • Your entries: the amount, whether it is income or expense, the optional note, and the date and time.
  • Your language and theme choice, kept on your phone only.

Where it is stored

Entries are saved on your phone first. They are then synced to a database hosted by Supabase and linked to your account. The database is set up so that each account can read and change only its own entries.

What the app does not include

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It asks Android for the internet permission only.

Files you export

PDF and Excel files are created on your phone. They leave your phone only when you choose to share them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry inside the app. To delete your whole account and everything linked to it, write to {{contact_email}}.
