Questions before you install

Where do I get the app?

This page gives you Daily Cash as an APK file, which you install directly on your Android phone. Tap the download button, open the file, and allow the install when Android asks.

Why does Android show a warning when I install?

Android shows a prompt for any app that does not come from the Play Store, and for apps from developers it has not seen often. The file is signed with the developer's own key. To continue, tap More details, then Install anyway. If a checksum is listed on this page, you can compare it with the file you downloaded to confirm it is the original.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, after the first login. You need internet once to create your account or log in. After that you can add, edit and delete entries offline, and they sync when your connection returns. A small cloud icon marks entries that are still waiting to sync.

Where is my data stored?

Your entries are saved on your phone and synced to a private database, linked to your account. Only the account you log in with can read them. The privacy page explains what is stored.

Can I fix a mistake?

Yes. Tap an entry to change its amount, note or type. Swipe an entry to the left to delete it, and tap Undo within five seconds if you change your mind.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

Yes. Log in with the same account on the other phone, and pull down on the home screen to refresh.

How do I keep a copy of my records?

Open Monthly summary, tap the share icon, and choose PDF or Excel. The file is created on your phone and you decide where to send it.

Can I use it in Bangla?

Yes. Switch between English and বাংলা in Settings. Numbers change to Bangla digits too, and there is a dark theme.
